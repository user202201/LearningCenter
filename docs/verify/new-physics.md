# Verification of the 13 newest physics-lab simulations

Simulations: `noise evap thermo straight diffuse lensexp internal resistance ohmlaw rmeasure meter emdev comms`
(models `src/phys/model-<id>.ts`, screens `src/phys/<id>.ts`, texts in `src/i18n-extra/physcontent.json` / `physlab.json`).

**Sources.** No outside source could be opened from the authoring sandbox (moe.gov.cn, zh.wikipedia.org and baike.baidu.com all
gave no connection through the proxy), so every fact was checked against the verifier's own knowledge of the 人教版 八年级上册 /
九年级全一册 textbooks (2012 edition, chapter numbers as in `docs/physics-coverage.md`), GB 3096 and standard constants. Nothing was
checked against the page of a textbook or the 2022 课程标准 text itself.

## Other things looked at without a change
* Dates / numbers that are teaching-model values and flagged as such in the lessons: the dB reduction of each noise measure, the evaporation-rate formula, the thermometer placement errors, eclipse tolerances (5 degrees about the node, beta limits 1.5 / 1 degree), the relay (150 Ω, 20 mA) and the speaker / microphone scales. They are plausible and never contradict a textbook statement.
* The Clausius-Clapeyron boiling point shown in the liquefaction part (and the critical temperature) goes beyond the 人教版 text; it is correct physics, kept (see open question 3).
* Wording of tasks, readouts and quiz explanations was read for colloquial or model-glitchy phrases; the ones listed above were the only clear cases.

`tests/e2e/82-physlab.cjs` needed no change of expected values: every changed text keeps its meaning for the checks (the "loud" task is solved with 100 dB in `SOLVE`). Result of the run is in the final report.
