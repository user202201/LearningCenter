# 化学 (junior-high chemistry): what the trainer covers and what it does not

Scope: 九年级化学, 人教版 / 2022 课标 as a general guide. Everything is written from memory (no textbook text is copied), ships inside the app
(`src/data/chem.json`, `src/chemlogic.ts`, `src/chemui.ts`, `src/chemfig.ts`) and works offline. Page: `#/science/chem` (science home -> 化学).
Progress (memory boxes 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 days, per item) stays on this device (`learn.chem.v1`); no child account is needed.

**act** 金属活动性 · **cls** 物质分类 · **ind** 指示剂与 pH · **gas** 气体的制取 · **ref** 查阅表格 (reference tables).

## Honest limits

- All content (157 reactions, 107 substances, phenomena, pH values, solubility numbers, gas data, explanations) was **written by a model from memory**. A teacher must
  proofread it: especially every phenomenon sentence, the conditions of each reaction (many have a "typical" and an "also possible" form), the solubility table cells
  (microsoluble CaSO4, Ag2SO4, MgCO3 are treated as "precipitate" when produced and as "not a reactant" when used, except Ca(OH)2), the solubility numbers, and the
  grade placement of reactions (the three levels are made by size of the equation, not by the textbook order).
- The 复分解 judge is a teaching rule (precipitate, gas or water; reactants soluble). Pairs that textbooks treat differently (a "-" cell of the table, a slightly
  soluble reactant) are marked ambiguous and are never asked.
- Valences: Fe3O4 (+8/3), NH4NO3 (two kinds of N) and organic carbon are not asked (no integer valence).
- The numbers of the stoichiometry use integer relative atomic masses (Cl = 35.5, no 63.5 for Cu): the school values.
- Not built: real solubility curves, gas volume, purity / yield, several-step word problems, identification (推断) and impurity-removal (除杂) problems, flowchart problems,
  drawings of apparatus other than the five of the gas preparation.
