# 化学 (junior-high chemistry): what the trainer covers and what it does not

Scope: 九年级化学, 人教版 / 2022 课标 as a general guide. Everything is written from memory (no textbook text is copied), ships inside the app
(`src/data/chem.json`, `src/chemlogic*.ts`, `src/chemui.ts`, `src/chemfig*.ts`) and works offline. Page: `#/science/chem` (science home -> 化学).
Progress (memory boxes 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 days, per item) stays on this device (`learn.chem.v1`); no child account is needed.

Tools (20 practice modes + the reference tables):
**bal** 配平 · **wr** 写方程式 · **val** 化学式与化合价 · **type** 反应类型与现象 · **calc** 根据方程式计算 · **sol** 溶液 · **dec** 复分解反应 · **act** 金属活动性 ·
**cls** 物质分类 · **ind** 指示剂与 pH · **gas** 气体的制取 ·
part 2: **curve** 溶解度曲线 · **calc2** 化学计算进阶 · **infer** 推断题 · **rem** 除杂与干燥 · **flow** 流程图题 · **lab** 实验基本操作 · **life** 化学与生活 · **atom** 原子与离子 ·
**prop** 物质的性质与用途 · **ref** 查阅表格 (solubility table, activity series, valences, substances, indicators / pH, 157 equations, first 20 elements).

How the part-2 questions are made (and checked): everything that can be computed is computed from the data (`chem.json`) by the same judges as part 1 and
**recomputed independently in `tests/e2e/81-chem.cjs`**: curve readings, solubility differences, densities, mixing, gas volumes, purity, yield, mass loss, the solution left after
a reaction, the unique answer of every 推断 / 除杂 question (every possibility is tried; a question is only asked when exactly one choice is right), the residues and filtrates of the
simulated flowcharts (an ion simulator, compared with the textbook results and a second simulator), electron shells, ions, general properties of acids / bases / salts.
Written question banks (lab safety, life, uses, atoms, gas drying ...) are **content**: four different choices and an explanation each, shown in `user` boxes (not translated).

## Honest limits

- All content (157 reactions, 107 substances, phenomena, pH values, solubility numbers, gas data, the banks of part 2: 38 + 15 + 27 + 9 + 4 + 7 questions, 13 ordering tasks, the
  class tables of foods / materials / elements / pollution, the 18 uses, the gas densities and acid densities) was **written by a model from memory**. A teacher must proofread it:
  especially every phenomenon sentence, the conditions of each reaction, the solubility table cells (microsoluble CaSO4, Ag2SO4, MgCO3 are treated as "precipitate" when produced
  and as "not a reactant" when used, except Ca(OH)2), the solubility numbers, the grade placement of reactions and questions, and the usual-ion list of the atoms.
- The 复分解 judge is a teaching rule (precipitate, gas or water; reactants soluble). Pairs that textbooks treat differently (a "-" cell of the table, a slightly soluble reactant OR product) are
  never asked: this includes every question of 推断 / 除杂 / 酸碱盐的通性 that would involve CaSO4, Ag2SO4, MgCO3 or Ca(OH)2 as a product.
- 推断 and 除杂 answers are unique **among the choices shown / for the assumptions stated** (reagents "in the right amount", flowchart reagents "in excess", slightly soluble = soluble in the
  flow simulator). A clever student may know a second possible reagent that is not offered; that is not an error of the question.
- The ion simulator of the flowcharts knows precipitation of "insoluble" cells, gas and water formation and metal displacement; it does not know complexes, amphoteric hydroxides
  (Al(OH)3 in excess NaOH), redox other than displacement, or partial reactions: only the three simulated flows are asked, and their results are checked against the textbook answers.
- Valences: Fe3O4 (+8/3), NH4NO3 (two kinds of N) and organic carbon are not asked (no integer valence). Atoms beyond Z = 20 are not drawn.
- The numbers of the stoichiometry use integer relative atomic masses (Cl = 35.5, no 63.5 for Cu): the school values. Gas volumes use the density at standard conditions given in the question
  (H2 0.09, O2 1.43, CO2 1.98 g/L), not the molar volume 22.4 L.
- The drawings (curves, atoms, apparatus, flowcharts) are simple vector drawings made in code, not textbook figures. The apparatus are recognisable but schematic.
- Not tested (see CLAUDE.md rule 56): real fingers / pencil on an iPad (ordering uses taps; the classification drag was tested with synthetic touch events), real devices, and above all the
  correctness of the content, which no test can prove.
