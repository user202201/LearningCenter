## 中文（简体）

**应用名称（最多 30 字）**：学习乐园

**副标题（iOS，最多 30 字）**：家长和老师的学习记录工具

**短描述（Google Play，最多 80 字）**：记录每周识字和成语，并配有数学、英语、科学等练习。数据保存在本机或你自己的私有仓库。

**完整描述**：

学习乐园是给家长和老师用的学习记录与练习工具。

识字
- 按周记录每个孩子学过的汉字和成语，每周 10 个字，重复的字会提醒。
- 字帖：按笔顺生成可打印的田字格练习页，一页 A4。
- 听写、手写笔顺练习、成语故事、古诗词、记忆曲线复习游戏。

其他科目
- 数学：口算和应用题练习、口诀表、错题本。
- 英语 / 法语：每周新单词、听写、每周阅读。
- 科学：每天几张知识卡、元素周期表、公式、地图、物理 / 化学 / 生物 / 地理实验室、天文。
- 编程：积木编程和入门文字编程。
- 另外有音乐（钢琴、鼓、吉他、节奏编辑、DJ）和一组小游戏。

数据与隐私
- 应用本身不收集任何数据，没有广告，没有统计，没有账号。
- 可以选择「仅在本设备」保存，或同步到你自己的私有 GitHub 仓库（需要自己申请访问令牌）。
- 可以离线使用；可以导出备份文件。
- 相机只用于扫描登录二维码和给孩子拍头像，图片不会上传。

内容说明
- 题库、故事、知识卡、古诗、地图等内容由模型根据记忆编写，尚未经过教师校对，可能有错误。请家长和老师在使用前自行核对。
- 朗读使用设备自带的语音；如果设备没有中文语音，可能没有声音。

**关键词（iOS，最多 100 字符）**：识字,汉字,成语,字帖,听写,学习记录,家长,老师,教学,练习

**更新说明（模板）**：
- 修复了一些问题，改进了稳定性。

**类别**：教育（Education）。**不选**儿童类别。

## English

**App name (max 30)**: Learning Center

**Subtitle (iOS, max 30)**: Study log for parents, teachers

**Short description (Google Play, max 80)**: Log weekly Chinese characters and idioms, with maths, language and science practice. Data stays on your device.

**Full description**:

Learning Center is a study-record and practice tool for parents and teachers.

Chinese characters
- Record the characters and idioms each child has learned, week by week, 10 characters a week, with a warning for repeats.
- Practice sheets: printable grid pages with stroke order, one A4 page.
- Dictation, finger writing of strokes, idiom stories, classical poems, a spaced-repetition review game.

Other subjects
- Maths: mental arithmetic and word problems, times table, a book of mistakes.
- English / French: new words every week, spelling dictation, weekly reading.
- Science: a few knowledge cards a day, periodic table, formulas, maps, physics / chemistry / biology / geography labs, astronomy.
- Coding: block programming and an introduction to text programming.
- Also music (piano, drums, guitar, beat maker, DJ) and a set of small games.

Data and privacy
- The app itself collects no data, shows no advertising, has no analytics and no account.
- Keep data "on this device only", or sync it to your own private GitHub repository (you create the access token yourself).
- Works offline; backups can be exported to a file.
- The camera is used only to scan the sign-in QR code and to take a picture for a child; pictures are not uploaded.

About the content
- Question banks, stories, knowledge cards, poems and maps were written by a model from memory and have not yet been proofread by a teacher; they may contain mistakes. Please check before use.
- Reading aloud uses the voices of your device; without a Chinese voice there may be no sound.

**Keywords (iOS, max 100 chars)**: chinese characters,idioms,hanzi,practice sheet,dictation,study log,parents,teachers

**What's new (template)**:
- Bug fixes and stability improvements.

**Category**: Education. **Not** the Kids / Families category.
